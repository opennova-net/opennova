# `art/onjo1/music/` sources

Every master in `masters/` is OpenNova's own, composed on 2026-10-14: sequenced from the CC0 samples
below (each note a sample played faster or slower to its pitch) and from synthesis (sine drones, band
noise), mixed, reverberated and levelled by throwaway scripts. Freesound files are each sound's HQ OGG
preview, the stream its page plays (the original upload needs a login); each page said Creative
Commons 0 when it was downloaded. No music of the original game is used: its banks were measured for
format, length, loudness and spectrum, and its scripts read for how they play, only (`README.md`).

## Source files

Every file is CC0 1.0, downloaded 2026-10-14 and kept as downloaded under `sources/`. The
orchestral samples are sgossner's uploads of the Versilian Community Sample Library; the demung
(a Javanese gamelan metallophone) bars set the music's tuning.

## How the masters are made

Each cue is a list of notes at 100 beats a minute in 4/4 (a bar 2.4 s), mixed at 44.1 kHz. A note
plays its sample faster or slower to its pitch, kept within a few semitones of the sample by octave
(a string or brass sample past that moves by an octave); a long note loops its sample's middle by
crossfade; every note fades in and out. The music is tuned to the demung's seven pelog bars: D 297.4,
Eb 318.3, F 351.6, G 398.4, A 445.5, Bb 474.1 and C 525.5 Hz, with the strings and brass on those
pitches by octave, and the major thirds and sixths of the win (F#, B, C#, E) in just intonation over
that D. A stereo reverb (filtered noise, 2.2 s) sits under every part. The mix is high-passed at
28 Hz, raised 3 dB under 160 Hz (the original's music is bass-heavy), levelled toward -20 dB RMS (the
calm beds `ONMENU02` and `ONMENU04` -21, the error bed -23) under a look-ahead limiter at -6.2 dBFS, resampled to 22050 Hz, kept at or under -6.1 dBFS
peak, TPDF-dithered and stored as 16-bit stereo. A bed that loops has its reverb tail folded onto its
start and is mastered and resampled as one turn of a ring, so it repeats without a seam.
