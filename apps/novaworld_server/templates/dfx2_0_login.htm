Create Account: If you do not have a NovaWorld account, you will need to create one in order to connect to NovaWorld. Clicking 'Create Account' will bring you to NovaWorld.com's easy breezy account creation system. Please note that you must have a valid email address in order to create a NovaWorld account. This email address is what will allow you to retrieve your account name or password in case you forget them. All information you fill out will not be shared nor sold to third parties. This is for NovaLogic's private use. Once you have created an account, please go back and login with the newly created account name and password. You may be requested to add an expansion key. A message will appear stating if you are successful. If a message appears stating the key is already in use, please contact Technical Support at http://www.novalogic.com/support.asp . Please state if the CD was new or used, include the CD Key in question, and your account name. If you encounter any other error messages during this process, please follow the steps above and contact Technical Support. Forgotten Name: If you have forgotten your account login name, enter the email address that you signed up for the account with. Your account login name will be sent to this email address. If you no longer are able to access the email account you signed up for the account with, please contact Technical Support at http://www.novalogic.com/support.asp. Please CD Key of your product to verify the account, and your account name. Forgotten Password: If you have forgotten your password, enter the email address that you signed up for the account with.Your password will be sent to this email address. If you no longer are able to access the email account you signed up for the account with, please contact Technical Support at http://www.novalogic.com/support.asp. Please CD Key of your product to verify the account, and your account name. Remember Me: If you do not wish to log in to NovaWorld every time, you may select the "Remember Me" option to be automatically logged in. Please only do this if you use a private computer. You will be held accountable for your account's actions, regardless of who is in front of the computer using, or abusing, it. Welcome to NovaWorld. Please log in or create an account.
